How it works

  • SRT, VTT, ASS and SBV in, SRT and VTT out, all parsed locally.
  • Constant shift for simple fixes and frame-rate rescale presets for growing drift.
  • Two-point anchor sync pins the first and last cue to known times.
  • Validation for overlaps, negative times and reading speed over 21 CPS, plus transcript export.
